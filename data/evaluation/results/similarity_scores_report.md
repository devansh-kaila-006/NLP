## Metric Explanations

### BLEU (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy)

**What it measures:** Precision-based metric that measures n-gram overlap between generated and reference text.

**How it works:**
- Counts matching n-grams (contiguous word sequences)
- BLEU-1: Single word matches
- BLEU-2: Two-word phrase matches
- BLEU-3: Three-word phrase matches
- BLEU-4: Four-word phrase matches (standard)

**Interpretation:**
- **0.0-0.1**: Poor overlap
- **0.1-0.2**: Fair overlap
- **0.2-0.3**: Good overlap
- **0.3-0.5**: Strong overlap
- **0.5+**: Very strong overlap

**Our Score (0.237):** Indicates fair to good phrase-level overlap. The generated answers capture technical terminology accurately while using varied explanatory phrasing.

---

### ROUGE (Recall-Oriented Understudy for Gisting Evaluation)

**What it measures:** Recall-based metric focusing on how much of the reference content is captured in the generated text.

**How it works:**
- **ROUGE-1**: Measures unigram (single word) overlap
- **ROUGE-2**: Measures bigram (two-word phrase) overlap
- **ROUGE-L**: Measures longest common subsequence (sentence-level structure)

**Interpretation:**
- **0.0-0.3**: Poor coverage
- **0.3-0.5**: Moderate to good coverage
- **0.5-0.7**: Good coverage
- **0.7+**: Strong coverage

**Our Scores:**
- **ROUGE-1 (0.468)**: Good word coverage - key terms and concepts are well captured
- **ROUGE-2 (0.414)**: Good phrase coverage - technical phrases preserved
- **ROUGE-L (0.462)**: Good structural coverage - logical flow maintained

**Why ROUGE excels:** ROUGE rewards semantic coverage and comprehensive content, which aligns with our RAG system's goal of providing thorough, informative answers.

### Length Ratio

**What it measures:** Ratio of generated answer length to reference answer length.
